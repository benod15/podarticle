Jared Isaacman: NASA's Moon Base by 2028, Optimus Robots on the Moon, and the Truth About UFOs

Guest: Jared Isaacman, NASA Administrator Originally aired: July 27, 2026 Runtime: 1h 27m

NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman joins Peter Diamandis and Dave Blundin to lay out a 2028 lunar south-pole landing, a phased plan for a permanent Moon base, and where Optimus-style robots and nuclear propulsion fit on the road to Mars. The conversation closes with Isaacman's on-record response to UAP disclosure questions.
