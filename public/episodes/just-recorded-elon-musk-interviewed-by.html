JUST RECORDED: Elon Musk Interviewed by JP Morgan's Jamie Dimon

Elon Musk speaks with Jamie Dimon at JP Morgan's headquarters to discuss SpaceX's transition to a public company to fund the deployment of over 100,000 communication satellites. Musk details plans for fully reusable Starship rockets, lunar bases, and the construction of space-based AI data centers powered by orbital solar energy. He also outlines the necessity of building domestic "Terafabs" to supply the chips required for advanced AI computing.
