Taylor Swift on Reclaiming Her Masters, Wrapping The Eras Tour, and The Life of a Showgirl | NHTV

Taylor Swift joins Jason and Travis Kelce to discuss reclaiming her master recordings, life on the Eras Tour, and her relationship with Travis. They cover her newfound appreciation for football, Travis's cameo on stage, and the upcoming release of her new album, The Life of a Showgirl. Swift also shares stories about fan traditions, baking sourdough bread, and ignoring social media algorithms.
