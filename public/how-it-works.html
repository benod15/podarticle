How PodArticle works

PodArticle turns a long podcast episode into a map: every section labeled, every timestamp checked against the actual transcript. You watch the parts you care about and skip the rest.

1

Find your episode

Type a show, guest, or topic into the box on the homepage and pick the episode from the results — no need to leave the site. Already have the YouTube link? Paste it instead. Messy links from the YouTube share sheet are fine; we tidy them automatically.

2

We map the episode

PodArticle pulls the episode's full caption track, then reads it end to end and builds a section-by-section map: a summary, the Top 5 moments worth your time, and a full section map covering the entire episode. Timestamps are matched against the transcript — never guessed.

Mapping your own episodes needs a free sign-in (Google or email). Browsing episodes other people have mapped never needs an account.

3

Watch only what matters

Tap any moment or section and the episode plays right on the page from that exact second. Found something a friend would love? Every moment has a small share icon — copy a link to that exact moment, post it, or text it. Whoever opens it lands on that moment.

Quick answers

Is it free? Yes — free while we grow, with no cap on how many episodes you map. We'll never pull a bait-and-switch; if pricing ever arrives, early users hear it from us first.

Does it work on any podcast? Any podcast on YouTube with captions. Very new episodes sometimes need a few hours before their captions are ready — if that happens, we'll tell you to try again later rather than show you a half-built map.

Do creators lose out? No. Every timestamp plays the original video on YouTube — views and ads stay with the creator. We're a discovery layer that sends episodes more viewers, not fewer.

Something broke. Email support@podarticle.com with the link and we'll take a look.
