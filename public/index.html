What's a podarticle?

A podarticle is the article form of a podcast — every section mapped, every moment timestamped, every claim checked against the transcript. Podcasts were made to be heard. Podarticles are made to be navigated. We think the word deserves a place in the dictionary, and we hope reading podarticles becomes your newest addiction.
