Where these actually get reach

1. Quote-tweet the show's own episode tweet — every big fantasy show announces new episodes; your map in a quote puts it in front of their audience (and shows often retweet). Use "Find the show's tweet" on any item.

4. Timing — draft season: 7–10pm ET. In-season: Sun night recaps, Tue waiver content.
