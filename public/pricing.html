Questions

What does it cost today?

Nothing. Every part of PodArticle is free at the moment — mapping episodes, reading the library, all of it. There is no card to enter and no trial that quietly ends.

What counts as one podarticle?

One analysis of one episode. Paste a YouTube podcast link and the map it generates — Top 5 moments plus the full section-by-section breakdown — is a podarticle. There is no limit on how many you can make.

When will you start charging?

Not for a while, and never without warning. We would rather have PodArticle in more hands than earn from it early. If plans do come back, the prices above are what we expect them to be, and anyone already using the site will hear from us first.

I subscribed earlier — what happens to my plan?

Nothing changes for you, and you are not being charged for something everyone else gets free. Email us and we'll cancel and refund the remainder of your term. You can also manage the subscription from the "Manage your subscription" link above.

How do I contact support?

Email support@podarticle.com and we'll get back to you within a day.

Why does PodArticle exist?

Because a three-hour podcast shouldn't require three hours. Every map links straight back into the YouTube video, so you watch the parts worth watching and keep your evening.
