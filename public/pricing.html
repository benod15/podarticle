Questions

What counts as one podarticle?

One analysis of one episode. Paste a YouTube podcast link and the map it generates — Top 5 moments plus the full section-by-section breakdown — is a podarticle. Your first five are on us.

Do episodes other people mapped count against my five?

No. Browsing the public library is always free. Only new analyses you request count toward your free five.

Can I cancel?

Monthly plans cancel anytime and stay active through the end of the billing period. Annual plans are billed once and run for the year. Manage or cancel your subscription from the "Manage your subscription" link above (visible when you're subscribed), and we'll email you a portal link after checkout too.

How do I contact support?

Email podarticle@gmail.com and we'll get back to you within a day.

Why does PodArticle exist?

Because a three-hour podcast shouldn't require three hours. Every map links back into the creator's YouTube video, so creators keep the viewership and you keep your evening.
